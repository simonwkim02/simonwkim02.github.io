Every autonomous robot trial in the paper, grouped by Pringles can position

Task: take a Pringles can out of a completely dark shelf. Six policies ran 70 autonomous trials at 16 Pringles can positions that are not in the training demonstrations. Every clip below is a policy controlling the robot on its own, never a teleoperated demonstration.

All six policies are fine-tuned from the π0.5 vision-language-action model, on the same 144 teleoperated demonstrations except PLA94 (94 demonstrations). ToF = the time-of-flight distance sensors in the robot hand's palm and two fingertips. Approach is judged from the robot's joint logs, as in the paper: OK when the hand is within 40 mm sideways of the Pringles can at the moment it has come down 50 mm, within 30 s. Grasped and pulled out are the operator's calls. By this rule, four trials that the operator stopped early as bad approaches count as approach OK (VLA144 at positions 2, 4 and 13, and the invalid-ToF policy at position 2), and one invalid-ToF trial that the operator let grasp (position 4) counts as a bad approach.

Friday: VPLA144, the invalid-ToF policy and VLA144 ran blinded under code names, in a random order at each position; the two ToF-only policies ran afterwards, not blinded, at the first three positions only. Saturday: the baseline VPLA144 ran not blinded, at the same positions in the same order.
